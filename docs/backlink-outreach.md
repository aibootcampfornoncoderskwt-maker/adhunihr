# Backlink and outreach kit

Links are earned off-site, so this is a working kit for the owner or a marketer. No bought links, link farms or paid-link networks: they risk a Google penalty.

## Order of work
1. **Profiles you control (week 1):** Google Business Profile, LinkedIn company page, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook and Instagram. Use the exact name, website and description below on all of them so the details match. Add `https://adhunihrsolutions.com/` as the website. (Needs real business details from the owner: address or service area, phone.)
2. **Directories and associations (weeks 1-4):** business directories in each target country, recruitment and staffing directories, Indian business councils and chambers of commerce in GCC cities, healthcare and energy trade associations. Only list what is true and consistent with the site.
3. **Content that earns links (ongoing):** the four guides on the site. Share them where employers and HR people read: LinkedIn, HR groups, industry forums. A guide that answers a real question gets cited.
4. **Partnerships (month 2 onward):** nursing and technical training institutes in India, hospital HR networks, industry newsletters. Offer a useful guide or a short expert quote, not a paid placement.
5. **Digital PR:** one original, factual piece per quarter (for example a plain checklist of licensing steps). Only publish data you can source.

## Consistent business description (copy exactly)
**Name:** Adhuni HR Solutions
**Website:** https://adhunihrsolutions.com/
**Short description:** Adhuni HR Solutions is a recruitment company connecting employers in Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman with candidates from India. Healthcare first, then oil, gas and energy.

## Email templates

Happy to answer questions or adjust anything you think is unclear.

[Sender name], Adhuni HR Solutions

Hello [Name],

We recruit nurses and technical staff for employers in the Gulf. We have written practical guides on licensing and on preparing for an overseas hiring process that your students and alumni may find useful: [URL]. If it fits, we would be glad to share them on your careers page or newsletter.

[Sender name]

## Rules
- Never claim placements, numbers, licences or awards that are not verified.
- Do not pay for links. Sponsored posts must be marked as sponsored.
- Keep a simple sheet: site, date contacted, result, link URL. Check in Search Console (Links report) each month.
- Disavow nothing unless Search Console shows a manual action.

## Lead conversion checklist
- Set `PUBLIC_BOOKING_URL` to show a "Book a call" link on the employer call-to-action.
- Set `PUBLIC_WHATSAPP_NUMBER` and `PUBLIC_PHONE_DISPLAY` when real.
- Set `PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` for Google Analytics 4, and update the privacy notice first to say analytics is used.
- Events the site sends: `employer_cta_click`, `candidate_cta_click`, `brief_builder_click`, `booking_click`, `email_click`, `phone_click`, `whatsapp_click`, `enquiry_sent`, `brief_sent`, `brief_pdf_downloaded`. In GA4 mark `enquiry_sent`, `brief_sent` and `booking_click` as key events.
- The hiring brief builder PDF is the lead magnet. Employers who finish it have already written a clear brief.
